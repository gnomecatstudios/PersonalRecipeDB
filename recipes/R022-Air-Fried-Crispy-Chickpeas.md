# Air-Fried Crispy Chickpeas

**Recipe ID:** R022  
**Course:** Snack  
**Method:** Air Fryer  
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes (plus 30–60 minutes drying)  
**Cook Time:** 15–22 minutes  
**Total Time:** 25–35 minutes (plus drying)  
**Yield:** 4 servings

Crunchy, high-fiber roasted chickpeas that make an inexpensive, portable snack requiring no refrigeration. Great for meal prep or keeping in a work bag.

## Ingredients

- 2 (15 oz / 425 g) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 2 teaspoons (10 g) olive oil
- 1 batch seasoning mix of choice

## Instructions

1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas thoroughly.
2. Spread the chickpeas on a clean kitchen towel or paper towels and pat completely dry. Remove as many loose skins as possible.
3. For the crispiest results, let the chickpeas air dry for **30–60 minutes**.
4. Transfer to a bowl and toss with the olive oil until evenly coated.
5. Preheat the air fryer to **390°F (200°C)**.
6. Arrange the chickpeas in a single layer in the air fryer basket.
7. Air fry for **15–22 minutes**, shaking the basket every **4–5 minutes**.
8. Cook until golden and crisp. The chickpeas will continue to crisp slightly as they cool.
9. Immediately toss with the selected seasoning blend while still warm.

## Storage

- Cool completely before storing.
- Store at room temperature in a loosely covered jar, paper bag, or container with the lid slightly ajar.
- Best enjoyed within **4–5 days**.
- To re-crisp, air fry at **390°F (200°C)** for **3–5 minutes**.

## Tips

- Dry the chickpeas as thoroughly as possible before cooking.
- Avoid overcrowding the air fryer basket.
- Add dry seasonings after cooking to reduce burning.
- Do not seal the chickpeas while they are still warm.
