# Chorizo Torta

**Recipe ID:** R029
**Yield:** 4 tortas
**Course:** Main
**Method:** Stovetop

## Ingredients

### Torta
- 454 g (1 lb) homemade Mexican chorizo
- 4 telera rolls
- 1 ball Oaxaca cheese, shredded
- 1 can pickled jalapeños
- 1/2 head iceberg lettuce, shredded
- Lard or oil, as needed

### Refried Beans
- 1 can pinto beans
- 2–3 tbsp lard
- 3 cloves garlic
- Salt, to taste

### Guacamole
- 1 avocado
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro
- 1 clove garlic, grated
- 1 tomato, finely diced
- 1 lime
- Salt, to taste

### Lime-Pickled Red Onions
- 1 red onion
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1/2 tbsp sugar
- Juice of 2 limes

## Instructions

1. **Make the lime-pickled red onions.** Thinly slice the red onion and place it in a bowl. Add the salt, sugar, and lime juice. Toss thoroughly and let sit for a few hours, stirring occasionally, until softened and bright pink-red.
2. **Make the refried beans.** Heat about half of the lard in a small pan over medium heat. Add the garlic cloves and cook until browned. Remove the garlic, leaving the lard in the pan. Add the browned garlic and remaining lard to a blender. Drain the pinto beans, add about two-thirds to the blender, and purée until smooth. Return the pan to medium heat, add the purée and remaining whole beans, season with salt, and stir to emulsify the fat. Simmer until thick and creamy, then set aside.
3. **Make the guacamole.** Grate the garlic into a bowl and add the lime juice. Halve and pit the avocado, mash the flesh, and add it to the bowl. Stir in the diced tomato and chopped cilantro. Season with salt and adjust with more salt or lime juice if needed.
4. **Cook the chorizo.** Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add a small amount of lard or oil, then the homemade chorizo. Cook while stirring and breaking it into small pieces. Allow it to brown and develop a little char without drying it out. Once fully cooked, taste and adjust the salt if necessary. Set aside.
5. **Toast the teleras and melt the cheese.** Slice each telera in half and gently hollow out some of the interior. Toast the rolls cut-side down on a hot skillet or griddle until golden brown. Remove from the pan. Place portions of shredded Oaxaca cheese directly on the hot pan and let the cheese melt and begin to caramelize. Press the cut side of each roll onto the melted cheese, then use a spatula to scrape underneath and release the caramelized cheese so it stays attached to the bread.
6. **Assemble.** Spread refried beans over the bottom half of each telera and top with cooked chorizo. Spread guacamole over the top half and add lime-pickled red onions, pickled jalapeños, and shredded iceberg lettuce. Close each torta, wrap in foil, cut in half, and serve.
