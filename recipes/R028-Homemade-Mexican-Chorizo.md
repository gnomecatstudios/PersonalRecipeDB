# Homemade Mexican Chorizo

**Recipe ID:** R028  
**Yield:** About 1 lb (454 g)  
**Course:** Ingredient / Protein  
**Method:** Stovetop

A richly seasoned fresh Mexican-style chorizo made with ground pork, dried chiles, warm spices, and vinegar.

## Ingredients

- 454 g (1 lb) ground pork
- 1 tbsp guajillo chile powder
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp New Mexico chile powder
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp dried garlic
- 1 tsp dried onion
- 1 tsp Mexican cinnamon
- 1 tsp Mexican oregano
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp cumin seed, ground
- 1 tsp coriander seed, ground
- 60 g (1/4 cup) vinegar
- 1–2 tbsp oil, as needed
- Salt, to taste
- Freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the guajillo chile powder, chili powder, New Mexico chile powder, cayenne, dried garlic, dried onion, cinnamon, oregano, thyme, ground cumin, and ground coriander.
2. Add the vinegar and 1 tablespoon of oil. Mix until a thick, tangy paste forms. Add additional oil if needed to loosen the mixture slightly. Season with salt and freshly cracked black pepper.
3. Add the ground pork and thoroughly work the seasoning mixture into the meat until evenly orange-red and completely incorporated.
4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2–4 hours. For deeper flavor, refrigerate overnight. The chorizo can also be cooked immediately if needed.
5. Preheat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add a small amount of oil or lard if needed, then add the chorizo.
6. Cook, stirring and breaking the pork into small pieces. Allow the meat to brown and develop some char around the edges without cooking it until dry.
7. Continue cooking until the pork reaches 160°F (71°C). Taste and adjust the salt if necessary.
8. Use anywhere fresh Mexican chorizo is called for.
