# Lemon-Glazed Persimmon Bars

**Recipe ID:** R015  
**Course:** Dessert  
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 25 minutes  
**Total Time:** 45 minutes, plus cooling  
**Yield:** One 10 × 15-inch pan  
**Tags:** `persimmon-bars`, `lemon-glaze`, `date-bars`, `walnut-dessert`, `fruit-dessert`, `holiday-baking`

Moist spiced persimmon bars with chopped dates and walnuts, finished with a simple lemon glaze.

## Ingredients

### Bars

- 1 large egg
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup oil
- 1 package (8 ounces) dates, finely chopped
- 1 1/2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 cup persimmon pulp
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 cup chopped walnuts

### Lemon Glaze

- 1 cup confectioners' sugar
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F (177°C)**. Grease and flour a **10 × 15-inch baking pan**.
2. In a large bowl, combine the egg, sugar, oil, and finely chopped dates. Mix well.
3. In a separate bowl, stir **1 1/2 teaspoons lemon juice** and the baking soda into the persimmon pulp.
4. In another bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves.
5. Add the dry ingredients to the date mixture alternately with the persimmon mixture, mixing well after each addition.
6. Stir in the chopped walnuts.
7. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan.
8. Bake for **25 minutes**, or until lightly browned and set.
9. Cool in the pan for **5 minutes**.
10. Stir together the confectioners' sugar and **2 tablespoons lemon juice** until smooth.
11. Spread the glaze over the warm bars.
12. Cool completely before cutting.

## Storage

Store covered at room temperature for up to **2 days** or refrigerate for up to **5 days**.

## Freezer Storage

Freeze unglazed bars in an airtight container for up to **2 months**. Thaw before glazing.

## Notes

- Omit the **1 1/2 teaspoons lemon juice in the batter** when using frozen persimmon pulp.
