# Pumpkin Biscuits

**Recipe ID:** R014  
**Course:** Breakfast / Side Dish  
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 11 minutes  
**Total Time:** 26 minutes  
**Yield:** About 10 biscuits  
**Tags:** `pumpkin-biscuits`, `buttermilk-biscuits`, `fall-baking`, `quick-bread`, `breakfast`, `side-dish`

Tender pumpkin biscuits with cinnamon and nutmeg, mixed in a food processor and baked hot for a quick rise.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/4 cup butter, cut into small pieces
- 3/4 cup fat-free buttermilk
- 1/2 cup canned pumpkin
- Cooking spray

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **450°F (232°C)**. Lightly coat a baking sheet with cooking spray.
2. Add the flour, sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, baking soda, salt, and nutmeg to a food processor. Pulse briefly to combine.
3. Add the butter and pulse until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
4. In a separate bowl, stir together the buttermilk and pumpkin until evenly combined.
5. Add the pumpkin mixture to the dry ingredients and pulse or stir only until the dough is just moistened.
6. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently just until it comes together.
7. Pat the dough to about **1 inch thick** and cut into biscuits.
8. Arrange the biscuits on the prepared baking sheet.
9. Bake for about **11 minutes**, or until risen and lightly browned.

## Storage

Store cooled biscuits in an airtight container at room temperature for up to **2 days** or refrigerate for up to **5 days**.

## Freezer Storage

Freeze baked biscuits in an airtight container for up to **2 months**.

## Reheating

Warm in a **350°F oven for 5–8 minutes** or microwave briefly until heated through.

## Notes

- The Orange-Honey Butter line on the original recipe was crossed out, so it is omitted here.
- Avoid overworking the dough so the biscuits stay tender.
