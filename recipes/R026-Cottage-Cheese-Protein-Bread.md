# Cottage Cheese Protein Bread

**ID:** R026  
**Course:** Bread / High-Protein  
**Method:** Baked  
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 35 minutes  
**Total Time:** 45 minutes, plus cooling  
**Yield:** 1 loaf

A simple high-protein bread made with cottage cheese, eggs, butter, and egg white powder. Let it cool completely before slicing for the best texture.

## Ingredients

- 4 large eggs
- 100 g butter, melted
- 300 g cottage cheese
- 120 g egg white powder
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon baking powder

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **160°C (320°F)**.
2. Line a baking tin with parchment paper and lightly grease it.
3. Add the eggs, melted butter, and cottage cheese to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
4. Pour the blended mixture into a bowl or jug for easier mixing and pouring.
5. Add the egg white powder, baking soda, and baking powder.
6. Whisk until completely combined and smooth. The batter should be fairly thick.
7. Pour the batter into the prepared tin and smooth the top.
8. Bake at **160°C (320°F) for 35 minutes**, or until set through the center.
9. Cool completely before slicing for the best results.

## Storage

- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
- Freeze individual slices for longer storage.
