# Rosemary Garlic Seasoning Mix

**Recipe ID:** R017  
**Category:** Seasoning Mix  
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Total Time:** 5 minutes  
**Yield:** About 3 tablespoons; seasons one 3-can batch of crispy chickpeas

A savory, herb-forward dry seasoning blend for crispy chickpeas, roasted vegetables, potatoes, chicken, or snack mixes.

## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 4 teaspoons dried rosemary, finely crushed
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Crush the dried rosemary between your fingers or with a mortar and pestle so the pieces adhere better to food.
2. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl or jar.
3. Stir or shake until evenly mixed.
4. Use the full batch to season approximately 8 portions of crispy chickpeas, or use about **1 teaspoon per portion** to taste.

## Storage

Store in a tightly sealed spice jar in a cool, dry cabinet for up to **6 months**. Shake before using.

## Notes

For air-fried chickpeas, add the seasoning immediately after cooking while the chickpeas are still hot. Avoid adding the blend before air frying because the garlic and rosemary can scorch.
