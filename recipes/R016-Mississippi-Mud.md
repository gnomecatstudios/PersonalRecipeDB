# Mississippi Mud

**Recipe ID:** R016  
**Source:** Marilna Nelson (modified)  
**Course:** Dessert  
**Prep Time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 10–15 minutes  
**Total Time:** About 1 hour, plus chilling  
**Yield:** 12 servings  
**Tags:** `mississippi-mud`, `chocolate-dessert`, `layered-dessert`, `pudding-dessert`, `pecan-crust`, `cream-cheese-dessert`

A chilled layered chocolate dessert with a pecan crust, sweet cream cheese filling, chocolate pudding, and whipped topping.

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup finely ground pecans

### Cream Cheese Layer

- 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 1/2 large container Cool Whip

### Chocolate Layer

- 2 large boxes instant chocolate pudding
- 3 cups milk

### Topping

- Remaining 1/2 large container Cool Whip

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F (177°C)**.
2. Mix the flour and ground pecans until evenly combined.
3. Press the mixture firmly into a **9 × 13-inch baking dish**.
4. Bake for **10–15 minutes**, then cool completely.
5. Beat the cream cheese and powdered sugar until smooth.
6. Fold in half of the Cool Whip.
7. Spread over the cooled crust and refrigerate for **15 minutes**.
8. Whisk the instant chocolate pudding and milk until smooth.
9. Spread the pudding over the cream cheese layer.
10. Refrigerate for **15 minutes**.
11. Spread the remaining Cool Whip over the top.
12. Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled before serving.

## Storage

Store covered in the refrigerator and serve cold.

## Notes

- The original crust included margarine, but it has been intentionally omitted from this version.
