# Everything Bagel Seasoning Mix

**Recipe ID:** R018  
**Category:** Seasoning Mix  
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Total Time:** 5 minutes  
**Yield:** About 3 tablespoons; seasons one 3-can batch of crispy chickpeas

A crunchy garlic, onion, sesame, and poppy seed blend for crispy chickpeas, eggs, toast, roasted vegetables, or snack mixes.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons plus 2 teaspoons everything bagel seasoning
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl or jar.
2. Stir or shake until evenly mixed.
3. Use the full batch to season approximately 8 portions of crispy chickpeas, or use about **1 teaspoon per portion** to taste.

## Storage

Store in a tightly sealed spice jar in a cool, dry cabinet for up to **6 months**. Shake before using.

## Notes

Everything bagel seasoning already contains salt, so taste the finished food before adding extra salt. Add this blend after air frying so the seeds do not burn.
