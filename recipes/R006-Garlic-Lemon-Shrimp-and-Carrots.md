# Garlic Lemon Shrimp and Carrots

**Recipe ID:** R006  
**Source:** Personal recipe  
**Yield:** 4 servings  
**Category:** Skillet  
**Meal Category:** Dinner  
**Tags:** `garlic-lemon-shrimp`, `shrimp-skillet`, `carrots`, `bell-pepper`, `high-protein`, `quick-dinner`, `dinner`

A quick garlic-lemon shrimp skillet with tender-crisp carrots and red bell pepper, served alone or over rice.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb (454 g) raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- Zest and juice of 1 lemon
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 12 oz (340 g) carrots, peeled and thinly sliced or cut into matchsticks
- 1 red bell pepper, sliced
- 2 cups cooked rice, optional for serving
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley, optional

## Instructions

1. Pat the shrimp dry. Season with the paprika, Italian seasoning, salt, and black pepper.

2. Heat 1 tablespoon of the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

3. Add the shrimp and cook for 1–2 minutes per side, just until pink and opaque. Transfer the shrimp to a plate.

4. Add the remaining tablespoon of olive oil to the skillet. Add the carrots and red bell pepper.

5. Cook the vegetables for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the carrots are tender-crisp.

6. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds, stirring constantly.

7. Return the shrimp to the skillet. Add the lemon zest and lemon juice, then toss until everything is evenly coated and heated through.

8. Garnish with parsley, if desired. Serve alone or over cooked rice.

## Ingredient Substitutions

- Use butter in place of some or all of the olive oil for a richer sauce.
- Use cauliflower rice instead of regular rice for a lower-carbohydrate serving option.
- Add crushed red pepper flakes for heat.

## Leftover and Meal-Prep Notes

Avoid overcooking the shrimp during the initial sear because it will heat again when returned to the skillet. Divide into airtight meal-prep containers with rice, if using.

## Refrigerator Storage

Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 3 days.

## Reheating

Reheat gently in a skillet over medium-low heat or microwave in short intervals until warmed through. High heat can make the shrimp rubbery.
