# German Pancake (Dutch Baby)

**Recipe ID:** R010  
**Source:** Woman's Day Kitchen  
**Course:** Breakfast  
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 12–15 minutes  
**Total Time:** 25 minutes  
**Yield:** 4 servings  
**Tags:** `german-pancake`, `dutch-baby`, `cast-iron`, `baked-breakfast`, `eggs`, `breakfast`

A classic German pancake baked in a hot cast-iron skillet until puffed, golden, and crisp around the edges.

## Ingredients

- 4 large eggs
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- Confectioners' sugar, for serving

### Optional Ingredients from Original Recipe

- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar *(crossed out on the original recipe)*
- 1/2 teaspoon grated lemon zest *(crossed out on the original recipe)*

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **425°F (218°C)**.
2. In a blender, combine the eggs, milk, flour, vanilla, and salt. Blend until smooth.
3. Heat a **9–10-inch cast-iron skillet** over medium heat.
4. Add the butter and allow it to melt completely.
5. Pour the batter into the hot skillet.
6. Transfer the skillet to the oven.
7. Bake for **12–15 minutes**, or until the pancake is puffed and golden brown.
8. Dust with confectioners' sugar before serving.

## Notes

- The granulated sugar and lemon zest were crossed out on the original recipe and are treated as optional.
- The pancake will puff dramatically while baking and naturally deflate as it cools.
- Best served immediately after baking.
