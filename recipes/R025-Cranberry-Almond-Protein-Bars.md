# Cranberry Almond Protein Bars

**Recipe ID:** R025  
**Course:** Snack  
**Method:** Oven  
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 18–22 minutes  
**Total Time:** 30–35 minutes  
**Yield:** 12 bars

Soft, chewy baked protein bars with dried cranberries, chopped almonds, and a light almond flavor.

## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients

- 240 g rolled oats
- 120 g oat flour
- 60 g vanilla protein powder
- 10 g baking powder
- 2 g salt

### Wet Ingredients

- 240 g plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 2 large eggs
- 80 g natural peanut butter
- 60 g honey
- 5 g vanilla extract
- 2 g almond extract (½ teaspoon)
- 60–120 ml milk, as needed

### Mix-ins

- 40 g dried cranberries
- 30 g chopped almonds

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Line an **8 × 8-inch (20 × 20 cm)** baking pan with parchment paper.
3. In a large bowl, mix the rolled oats, oat flour, protein powder, baking powder, and salt.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the Greek yogurt, eggs, peanut butter, honey, vanilla extract, and almond extract.
5. Add the wet mixture to the dry ingredients and stir until combined.
6. Add milk gradually until the batter resembles thick brownie batter.
7. Fold in the dried cranberries and chopped almonds.
8. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared pan.
9. Bake for **18–22 minutes**, until the center is just set.
10. Cool completely before slicing into 12 bars.

## Storage

- Store at room temperature for 2–3 days.
- Refrigerate for up to 1 week.
- Freeze individually wrapped bars for up to 3 months.
