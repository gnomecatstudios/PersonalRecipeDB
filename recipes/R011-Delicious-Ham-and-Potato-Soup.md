# Delicious Ham and Potato Soup

**Recipe ID:** R011  
**Source:** Allrecipes, submitted by ELLIE11  
**Course:** Soup  
**Prep time:** 20 minutes  
**Cook time:** 25 minutes  
**Total time:** 45 minutes  
**Yield:** 8 servings  
**Tags:** `ham-and-potato-soup`, `potato-soup`, `ham-soup`, `creamy-soup`, `one-pot`, `dinner`

A creamy ham and potato soup with celery, onion, and a simple butter-and-flour milk base.

## Ingredients

- 3 1/2 cups peeled and diced potatoes
- 1/3 cup diced celery
- 1/3 cup finely chopped onion
- 3/4 cup diced cooked ham
- 3 1/4 cups water
- 2 tablespoons chicken bouillon granules
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- 1 teaspoon ground white or black pepper, or to taste
- 5 tablespoons butter
- 5 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 2 cups milk

## Instructions

1. Combine the potatoes, celery, onion, ham, and water in a stockpot. Bring to a boil, then cook over medium heat until the potatoes are tender, about 10–15 minutes. Stir in the chicken bouillon, salt, and pepper.
2. In a separate saucepan, melt the butter over medium-low heat. Whisk in the flour with a fork and cook, stirring constantly, until thick, about 1 minute.
3. Slowly stir in the milk, adding it gradually to prevent lumps. Continue stirring over medium-low heat until thickened, about 4–5 minutes.
4. Stir the milk mixture into the stockpot. Cook until the soup is heated through, then serve immediately.
