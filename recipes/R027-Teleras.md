# Teleras

**Recipe ID:** R027  
**Category:** Mexican Bread  
**Method:** Baked  
**Prep Time:** 30 minutes, plus rising  
**Cook Time:** 18–20 minutes  
**Total Time:** About 2 hours 15 minutes, or overnight with cold rise  
**Yield:** 6 rolls  
**Tags:** `teleras`, `mexican-bread`, `torta-roll`, `sandwich-roll`, `yeast-bread`, `stand-mixer`

Classic Mexican torta rolls with a soft, pillowy interior, lightly floured crust, and the traditional double ridges across the top. An overnight rise gives the dough even more flavor.

## Ingredients

- 7 g (2 1/4 teaspoons / 1 packet) instant or active dry yeast
- 302 g (1 1/3 cups) lukewarm water
- 25 g (2 tablespoons) sugar
- 420 g (3 cups) all-purpose flour
- 25 g (2 tablespoons) melted fresh-rendered pork lard or vegetable oil, plus more for oiling the bowl
- 2 teaspoons salt

## Instructions

1. In the bowl of a stand mixer, stir together the yeast, lukewarm water, and sugar. If desired, let stand for a few minutes until clearly bubbly to confirm the yeast is active.
2. Add the flour, melted lard or oil, and salt.
3. Fit the mixer with the dough hook and mix on low speed for 1–2 minutes, until mostly combined.
4. Increase to medium speed and knead for about 8 minutes, until the dough is smooth, elastic, and pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
5. Lightly oil the dough and bowl. Turn the dough so it is lightly coated all over. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled, about 1 hour.
6. For deeper flavor, instead refrigerate the covered dough overnight. Before shaping, let it warm at room temperature for about 2 hours.
7. Press the risen dough down to release excess gas. Turn it onto a lightly floured work surface and divide into 6 equal pieces.
8. Roll each piece into a ball, lightly flour, cover with a towel, and rest for about 15 minutes.
9. Flatten each ball into an oval about 5 × 3 inches. Place on a parchment- or silicone-lined baking sheet, spacing the rolls a couple of inches apart.
10. Using a floured chopstick or narrow wooden spoon handle, press two evenly spaced deep indentations lengthwise into each oval, almost all the way through the dough.
11. Lightly flour the tops and cover with a towel.
12. Heat the oven to 400°F. Let the rolls proof until almost doubled, about 30 minutes.
13. Bake for 18–20 minutes, until golden brown.
14. Cool on a rack before slicing for tortas.

## Variations

- Use vegetable oil instead of lard for a pork-free version.
- Use the overnight refrigerated rise for a more developed bread flavor.

## Substitutions

- Active dry yeast and instant yeast can both be used at the same 7 g quantity.
- Neutral vegetable oil can replace rendered pork lard 1:1 by weight.

## Storage

- **Room temperature:** Store airtight for up to 2 days.
- **Refrigerator:** Not recommended because refrigeration can stale bread faster.
- **Freezer:** Freeze well wrapped for up to 2 months; thaw at room temperature and refresh briefly in a warm oven if desired.

## Nutrition

- Nutrition varies depending on whether lard or vegetable oil is used.

## Equipment

- Stand mixer with dough hook
- Baking sheet
- Parchment paper or silicone baking mat
- Chopstick or narrow wooden spoon handle
- Cooling rack

## Notes

- The two deep lengthwise grooves are the characteristic telera shape.
- For the best flavor, use the overnight cold-rise option.
- These rolls are especially suited to Mexican tortas because they are soft enough to compress around the filling without falling apart.
