# Garlic Parmesan Seasoning Mix

**Recipe ID:** R020  
**Category:** Seasoning Mix  
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Total Time:** 5 minutes  
**Yield:** About 3 tablespoons; seasons one 3-can batch of crispy chickpeas

A savory, cheesy garlic blend for crispy chickpeas, popcorn, roasted potatoes, vegetables, or breadsticks.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons plus 2 teaspoons finely grated shelf-stable Parmesan cheese
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon dried parsley
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl or jar.
2. Stir or shake until evenly mixed.
3. Use the full batch to season approximately 8 portions of crispy chickpeas, or use about **1 teaspoon per portion** to taste.

## Storage

If made with shelf-stable grated Parmesan, store tightly sealed in a cool, dry cabinet and follow the cheese package's storage guidance. If made with freshly grated Parmesan, refrigerate and use within **5 days**.

## Notes

For a portable, unrefrigerated chickpea snack, use shelf-stable grated Parmesan and add the blend after air frying. The cheese may soften the chickpeas slightly during extended storage, so this flavor is best within **3 days**.
