# Taco Salad

**Recipe ID:** R013  
**Course:** Main Dish  
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 15 minutes  
**Total Time:** 30 minutes  
**Yield:** 4 servings

A hearty, protein-packed taco salad with seasoned lean beef, crisp vegetables, beans, and a simple Greek yogurt salsa dressing.

## Ingredients

### Taco Meat

- 1 lb (454 g) lean ground beef (90–93%)
- 1 small onion, diced (150 g)
- 3 cloves garlic, minced (10 g)
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste (32 g)
- 2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 cup water (60 ml)

### Salad

- 1 large head romaine lettuce, chopped (about 8 cups)
- 2 medium Roma tomatoes, diced (about 300 g)
- 1 cup cucumber, diced (150 g)
- 1 cup canned black beans, drained and rinsed (170 g)
- 1 cup corn kernels (150 g)
- 1/4 cup red onion, thinly sliced (40 g)
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese (56 g)
- 1 avocado, diced (optional)
- 1–2 cups shredded green cabbage (optional)

### Dressing

- 3/4 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt (170 g)
- 1/4 cup salsa (60 g)
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1 teaspoon taco seasoning (optional)

### Optional Toppings

- Crushed baked tortilla chips
- Fresh cilantro
- Jalapeños
- Extra salsa
- Hot sauce

## Instructions

1. Brown the ground beef and diced onion in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Stir in the tomato paste, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, and black pepper.
4. Add the water and simmer for 3–5 minutes, until the mixture thickens.
5. While the meat cooks, prepare the lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, beans, corn, red onion, cheese, avocado, and optional cabbage.
6. Whisk together the Greek yogurt, salsa, lime juice, and optional taco seasoning.
7. Divide the lettuce among four bowls.
8. Top with the vegetables, beans, corn, cheese, avocado, optional cabbage, and warm taco meat.
9. Drizzle with dressing and add any desired toppings.

## Meal Prep

- Store the taco meat, vegetables, and dressing separately.
- Assemble just before eating to keep the lettuce crisp.
- Refrigerate for up to 4 days.
