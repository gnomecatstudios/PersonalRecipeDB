# Chili-Lime Seasoning Mix

**Recipe ID:** R019  
**Category:** Seasoning Mix  
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Total Time:** 5 minutes  
**Yield:** About 3 tablespoons; seasons one 3-can batch of crispy chickpeas

A bright, smoky, mildly spicy dry blend for crispy chickpeas, corn, roasted vegetables, chicken, or snack mixes.

## Ingredients

- 4 teaspoons chili powder
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons lime zest powder or crystallized lime
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl or jar.
2. Stir or shake until evenly mixed.
3. Use the full batch to season approximately 8 portions of crispy chickpeas, or use about **1 teaspoon per portion** to taste.

## Storage

Store in a tightly sealed spice jar in a cool, dry cabinet for up to **6 months**. Shake before using.

## Notes

Use a dry lime ingredient for shelf-stable storage. Fresh lime juice adds moisture and will soften crispy chickpeas. Add the blend after air frying while the chickpeas are still hot.
