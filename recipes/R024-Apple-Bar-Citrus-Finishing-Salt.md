# Apple Bar Citrus Finishing Salt

**Recipe ID:** R024  
**Course:** Condiment  
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Dry Time:** 20 minutes–24 hours  
**Yield:** About 1/2 cup

A bright citrus finishing salt with warm cinnamon and vanilla, designed specifically for sprinkling over apple protein bars.

## Ingredients

- 100 g flaky sea salt
- 4 g finely grated lemon zest (about 1 lemon)
- 2 g finely grated orange zest (about 1/2 orange)
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon (about 1 g)
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla powder, or seeds from 1/4 vanilla bean

## Instructions

1. Wash and thoroughly dry the lemon and orange.
2. Finely zest only the colored outer peel, avoiding the bitter white pith.
3. Mix the flaky sea salt, lemon zest, orange zest, cinnamon, and vanilla until evenly combined.
4. Spread the mixture in a thin layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
5. Dry completely using either method:
   - Air dry for 12–24 hours.
   - Bake at 170°F (75°C) for 20–30 minutes, stirring halfway through.
6. Cool completely and break up any clumps.
7. Store in an airtight jar away from moisture.

## Use

Sprinkle a small pinch over apple protein bars immediately after baking while they are still warm so the flakes adhere.

## Storage

Store airtight at room temperature for up to 6 months, provided the citrus zest is fully dried before sealing.
